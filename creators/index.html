Who it's for

Small and growing chess creators on TikTok, YouTube, Twitch and Instagram. You don't need a big audience. If you post games, puzzles, rating climbs or "rate my blunder" content, it'll fit.

The hook: "let the AI roast your game"

Play a game, open the AI coaching report, and react to it. Chess AI's coach goes through your mistakes move by move: the hung piece, the missed fork, the moment the evaluation fell apart. A few formats that work:

  • Roast my game. Read the coach's verdict on your worst loss of the week out loud.
  • Viewer games. Have chat send games, run them through the review and react live.
  • Climb series. Play the adaptive bot at your level and let the coach track what you keep getting wrong.

What your viewers get

A free month of Chess AI Diamond, our top plan: AI coaching reports on every game from our deepest model, plus a full player review across all their games. They redeem your code on the App Store (it works even before they install the app). After the free month it renews at the regular price unless they cancel.

What you earn

15% of our net revenue (what we get after Apple's cut) on every payment from each subscriber who used your code, for their first 12 months. Monthly and yearly plans both count, and so do renewals.

For example: a viewer on Diamond Monthly ($12.99) earns you about $1.65 a month, or roughly $20 over the year. Fifty of them is about $80 a month.

We pay monthly via PayPal once your balance reaches $50. Anything below that rolls over to the next month.

How to join

  1. Email raj@getchessai.com with a link to your channel.

  3. Put the code or link in your videos, bio, pinned comment or chat. We send a short monthly summary and pay out via PayPal.

FAQ

How do you know someone came from me?

Your code is a real App Store offer code. When someone redeems it, Apple tells us which code started the subscription. There are no cookies or tracking links, and it still counts if they install the app days later.

What counts as net revenue?

What we actually receive for a subscription after Apple's commission. Refunded payments don't count.

When does the 12 months start?

When your viewer redeems your code. Every payment they make in the 12 months after that earns you 15%. The free month itself is free, so it doesn't pay anything.

When and how do I get paid?

Once a month via PayPal, as soon as you've earned at least $50. We include a summary of how many people used your code and what they paid.

Is there a minimum follower count?

No. We care more about whether your audience plays chess than how big it is.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. Chess AI is on iPhone and iPad, so the code is for viewers using an Apple device.
